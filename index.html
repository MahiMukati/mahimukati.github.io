Mohammad Mahi Ali Mukati

Software developer · Computer Science co-op at the University of Toronto

I’m Mahi, a software developer studying Computer Science in the Honours Bachelor of Science co-op program at the University of Toronto, with graduation expected in 2028. I’m also pursuing minors in Business, Science, and Entrepreneurship, and Geographical Information Systems. My work spans systems programming, desktop applications, and tools that connect software with machine learning.

I’m currently the Software Developer and Unity Integration Lead on an AMD–University of Toronto experiential industry project, working on bringing AMD Schola’s reinforcement learning tools from Unreal Engine to Unity. I’ve led the C# interface design for environments, agents, sensors, actuators, and policies; built a Unity Editor configuration prototype; and established the project’s CI/CD pipeline. Technical reviews with AMD engineers help shape the requirements for Python training and local inference.

Previously, I worked as a Software Developer and QA Tester at REP, supporting Geist, an AI-powered ideation platform. I tested features and releases, helped resolve defects, coordinated a beta-testing community, and translated feedback from users and prospective clients into product requirements.

My projects include MYSH, a Unix-like shell in C; Jraw, a JavaFX drawing application; and ImpulseGuard, a browser extension MVP that placed fourth among 32 teams at the Ibtikar Case Competition in Waterloo.

Outside software, I serve as Ontario Regional Vice Representative for Charity Week with Islamic Relief Canada. I led 30 outreach coordinators supporting more than 70 institutions during a campaign that raised over CA$500,000 in one week. I also serve on UofT Mississauga Campus Council, am a National Council of Canadian Muslims campus liaison, and spoke at TEDx in 2023.
